System Architecture & Pipeline

This platform integrates clinical EHR lab records, vocal frequency time-series, glycemic panels, cardiac markers, and medical spatial imaging into a unified single-dashboard diagnostic engine.

1. Renal Biomarkers

Processes 16 clinical parameters including Serum Creatinine, Albumin, & Hemoglobin via Random Forest Ensemble.

2. Acoustic Tremor

Evaluates vocal fundamental frequencies (MDVP Fo/Fhi/Flo), Jitter, & Shimmer via XGBoost Gradient Boosting.

3. Glycemic Panel

Analyzes Fasting Glucose, Serum Insulin, BMI, & Diabetes Pedigree score via Random Forest Classifier.

4. Cardiac Panel

Evaluates Resting BP, Serum Cholesterol, Max Heart Rate, & ST Depression via Gradient Boosting Classifier.

5. Nuclear Morphometry

Evaluates 10 mean nuclear characteristics (Radius, Perimeter, Concavity, Compactness) via Random Forest WDBC model.

6. Spatial CNN Scan

Extracts 2D feature maps from lung X-ray & CT scans for pulmonary opacity and consolidation scoring.
